The short version read this first

The reskin replaced a single olive-brass accent with a proper gold scale, and turned an all-dark site into one that alternates dark ink with warm cream. US and UK are colour-identical — one palette covers both.

For email, four values do most of the work: ink #111820, cream #FFF9F5, gold #7C5F26 for buttons, and light gold #D2AD75 for small accents. Georgia for headings, Montserrat (falling back to Arial) for everything else.

Gold system the reskin's headline change

The old site had one gold. The reskin has a scale plus two gradients — a deep one for buttons and a pale one for decorative fills. Email should use the flat #7C5F26; the gradients are web-only.

Loose ends worth settling before the flows are built

None of these break the site, but each one gives Austen a different answer to "what does a BOLLE button look like". Settling them now means the emails match whichever answer you pick.

  1. 01

    The primary button has three different looks

    The hero uses the gold gradient with white Georgia at 2.8px tracking. Homepage cards use the same gradient with white Assistant at 12px. Product pages use flat #7C5F26 with cream Montserrat. Same button, three treatments.

    Suggested canonical: flat #7C5F26, label #FFF9F5, Montserrat 600 / 12px / uppercase / 1.2px, 8px radius, 13×20px padding — the product-page version. It's the one that converts, it survives Outlook, and it's the easiest to reproduce in Klaviyo.

  2. 02

    The footer Subscribe button is a fourth look

    It's #D2AD75 with an ink label — noticeably lighter than every other CTA on the site, and #D2AD75 isn't in the --bolle-* token set at all despite also being used for the cart badge and ghost-button borders.

    Either promote #D2AD75 to a real token as the light-gold accent, or bring the Subscribe button onto #7C5F26. Since email is mostly signup and nurture CTAs, this one matters for Austen.

  3. 03

    The Klaviyo signup popup matches neither site

    The "Join the Club" form renders on champagne #E5D4B8 in Poppins — a colour and a typeface that appear nowhere else in the reskin.

    Quick win: this is a Klaviyo-side fix, so it's already Austen's territory. Move it to cream #FFF9F5 with Georgia headings and a #7C5F26 button and the whole signup-to-welcome journey lines up.

  4. 04

    A stray webfont is loading

    Assistant is fetched and applied to six elements, and font-faces for Marcellus, Playfair Display and Work Sans are declared but never used. Leftovers from the previous theme or an app.

    Fix: point those six elements at Montserrat and drop the unused faces. Faster page, and the type system becomes two faces you can state in one line.

  5. 05

    Two contrast traps to avoid in email

    White text on light gold #D2AD75 is 2.11:1 — unreadable. Gold #7C5F26 as text on ink is 3.0:1, fine at display size but not for body copy.

    Rules of thumb: on #D2AD75 always use ink #111820. For gold text on dark, use #D2AD75 (8.49:1) or #EFDCBD (13.31:1) instead of the base gold.

Two things to look at separately

The UK "poured at" strip is listing US venues — San Francisco and Sonoma appear on bolledrinks.co.uk. And the UK carries a legacy --bolle-radius: 14px token from the old theme while the US carries --bolle-chrome-h; neither site has both. Harmless, but they'll drift further apart with each edit.
